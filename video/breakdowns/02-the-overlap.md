## The hook

Brisbane completed more than twice as many passes as Perth, 502 to 240, and lost 1-0. Perth's winner came from their right-back, who started the move in his own half and got the ball 5 m from Brisbane's byline.

## The idea

An overlap is a run around the outside of the team-mate on the ball. It puts two attackers against one full-back. If the full-back goes to the ball, the runner is free. If he stays with the runner, the man on the ball is free. The run is 50 m or more, so it only works if nobody travels with the runner.

## Why this moment

- Risdon's overlap is the longest of the match's 14 (53 m) and the only one that led to a goal. Across the seven matches there are 107 overlaps, and only 2 led to goals.
- Perth made a triple substitution at 62:22: Carluccio, Ngo and Blair came on. Six minutes later, all three are in the winning move.

## The breakdown

Perth are white and attack right to left; Brisbane are orange.

   - Caption: *"Watch Perth's right-back. He starts in his own half."*
2. **68:11–68:16 · Left, then back inside.** Warland, then Williams. The Event Map draws Perth's first runs: Ngo into the half-space, Blair in behind, Carluccio ahead of the ball.
   - Caption: *"Three runs before the ball even goes right."*
3. **68:16.0–68:23.3 · The overlap.** Williams finds Carluccio on the right, and Risdon goes around him: 53 m at an average of 26 km/h. Brisbane's left winger Jelacic runs back too, but from 68:10 Risdon covers 81 m to his 53 m and finishes 14 m ahead of him.
   - Caption: *"81 metres. Brisbane's winger can't stay with him."*
4. **68:18.0–68:21.7 · Carluccio fixes the full-back.** Carluccio carries 16 m straight at goal (3 m infield). Burke-Gilroy and O'Shea both go to him: when he passes, they're 4.3 m and 2.5 m away. Risdon is 7.9 m from the nearest defender.
   - Caption: *"Two defenders on the ball. Nobody on the runner."*
5. **68:23.3 · Five in the box.** Risdon receives 5 m from the byline. Perth have five in the box (Risdon, Blair, Carluccio, Ngo, Williams) against six Brisbane outfield players. The Event Map shows four runs to meet the cross at once, and Ngo's is the most threatening run of the match (xThreat 0.40).
   - Caption: *"Four runners for the cross."*
6. **68:25.3 · Goal.** Risdon floats it across the six-yard box. Williams, arriving late from the edge of the area, finishes at the back post. Hingert is 4 m from him.
   - Caption: *"Williams, arriving late. 1-0."*

## The lesson

Brisbane defended the ball, not the run. Two players went to Carluccio and nobody went with Risdon. When a full-back overlaps, someone has to travel with him the whole way, and Jelacic didn't get there.

## Why it shows the value

- **The whole move in one frame:** the Event Map draws all 9 runs by 5 Perth players in 15 seconds, so the work off the ball is as visible as the ball.
- **The match in one picture:** the Pass Network shows Brisbane's thick lines across the back and a striker, Waddingham, in 9 of their 502 passes over 102 minutes. Perth didn't need the ball; they needed one move.
- **A clip for two players:** a full-back and a winger can see exactly when to go, and who should have travelled with the runner.
