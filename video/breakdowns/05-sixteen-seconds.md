## The hook

Victory had all ten outfield players back to defend a corner. Sixteen seconds later they scored at the other end, and two of them ran about 90 metres to do it.

## The idea

Rest defence is the players you leave behind when you commit numbers forward, ready for the moment you lose the ball. At a corner, teams keep two or three players out of the box to stop the counter. Western United kept three, and Victory had nobody up at all. On paper, that's safe.

## Why this moment

When Victory won the ball, Pitch Control gave Western United all of their own half. Twelve seconds later, it was a goal. A single frame said "safe"; playing it through shows the space two sprints create.

## The breakdown

Victory are navy and attack left to right; Western United are white.

1. **36:40.0 · The corner.** Garuccio takes it from the top-left. All ten Victory outfield players are within 13 m of their own goal line. Western United have six in the box, Vickery and Bozinovski on its edge, and Russell 38 m out. Their keeper, Sutton, is 30 m off his line.
   - Caption: *"Western United's corner. Three stay out to guard against the counter."*
2. **36:44.2 · Victory win it.** It's cleared to Valadon at the edge of the box. Pitch Control: Victory control 11% of the pitch and none of Western United's half. Arzani (7) and Fornaroli (10) are at the edge of their own box.
   - Caption: *"Victory win it. Western United own their whole half."*
3. **36:45.5–36:50.2 · The carry.** Valadon's first-time pass finds Fornaroli, and he carries it 37 m. Vickery chases him from 6.7 m to 1.9 m, but Fornaroli lets it go first. Meanwhile Arzani sprints in behind. Pitch Control turns: Victory go from 17% of the pitch to 51%.
   - Caption: *"One carry, and the pitch turns navy."*
4. **36:50.2–36:53.2 · Two against one.** Fornaroli's 35 m pass finds Arzani's run (72 m at an average of 29 km/h). Russell goes to Arzani and is 1.7 m from him when he receives. Fornaroli keeps running, 17 m clear of Russell.
   - Caption: *"Russell takes the ball. Fornaroli keeps going."*
5. **36:55.3–36:56.3 · Goal.** Arzani carries towards the byline and plays it 15 m across to Fornaroli, who scores. Vickery, who chased the whole way, is 3.9 m from him.
   - Caption: *"16 seconds after the corner."*
6. **The totals.** From the interception to the goal took 12.1 s. Arzani ran 94 m and Fornaroli 89 m. By the shot, Victory control 43% of Western United's half.
   - Caption: *"Arzani 94 m. Fornaroli 89 m. 12 seconds."*

## The lesson

Three against none sounds safe, but rest defence has to stop the first carry. Nobody stopped Fornaroli's, so by the time the ball reached Western United's half, the last man, Russell, was facing two.
